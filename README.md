# Flowcrate

**Dig deeper. Save what you find. Follow the thread.**

Flowcrate is a music discovery and organization tool built around the connections between tracks, artists, DJs, sets, and labels.

Start with a DJ whose taste you trust, a set you love, or a track already sitting in your library. Flowcrate helps you follow those connections outward — then gives you a place to save what you find and organize it into a library you can actually use.

## What you can do

### Discover through DJs and sets

Explore the music DJs are actually playing.

Flowcrate connects tracks with the sets and mixes where they've appeared, so you can dig into:

- which DJs have played a track
- how often it appears across mixes
- where tracks appear within a set
- related artists and labels
- other music connected to the DJs you follow

Follow artists and DJs whose taste you trust, then use Discover and Radar to keep digging through the music around them.

### Build your own music library

Save tracks you find anywhere in Flowcrate and build a library around your own taste.

Where available, Flowcrate enriches tracks with context such as:

- artist
- release and label
- genre
- BPM and key
- sets and mixes where the track has appeared
- links for exploring the artist, release, or label further

Your library isn't just a list of tracks — it's a way to remember what you've found, understand the connections around it, and build toward the music you want to play.

### Organize with crates

Create crates for whatever makes sense to you:

- a particular sound
- a mood
- tracks that work together
- music for a future set
- a scene, label, or era
- completely personal categories that only make sense to you

Flowcrate is designed around the way DJs and music obsessives actually dig: loosely at first, then gradually turning discoveries into something useful.

### Explore artists and labels

Discovery shouldn't stop once you've identified a track.

Flowcrate connects music back to the artists and labels behind it whenever that information is available, making it easier to explore where the music came from, find related work, and support the people making it.

Artist and label discovery are still being expanded as Flowcrate grows.

## Current features

Flowcrate currently includes:

- Discover
- DJ and artist following
- Radar
- personal track library
- crates
- DJ set and tracklist exploration
- track appearances across mixes
- artist and label connections
- track metadata enrichment
- crate and full-library CSV export

## Status

Flowcrate is an independent project in active development. Features and data coverage are still evolving.

The long-term goal is to help people discover music through the DJs, artists, labels, and scenes they care about, save what they find, organize it in ways that make sense to them, and eventually turn those discoveries into sets of their own.
